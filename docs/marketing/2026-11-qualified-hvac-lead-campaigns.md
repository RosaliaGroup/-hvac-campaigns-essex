### Search-term hygiene and location
Use serviceable NJ counties and Google's physical-presence location setting where available; avoid targeting all of the United States. Start with phrase/exact matching and evaluate real search terms before expansion. Review negatives: jobs, careers, salary, training, DIY, how-to manual, parts only, wholesale, used equipment, automotive AC, free download, school, and consumer-only terms in commercial ad groups. Do not apply broad negatives without checking that they would not exclude real buyers. Avoid guaranteed same-day response, unverified savings, specific rebate amounts or unsupported case studies.
