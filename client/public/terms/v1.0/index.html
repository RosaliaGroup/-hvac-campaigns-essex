Part I · 10-Year Labor Warranty

Mechanical Enterprise LLC provides a 10-Year Limited Labor Warranty on qualifying residential HVAC installations, subject to the terms below.

1 Warranty eligibility

To maintain the full 10-Year Limited Labor Warranty, the homeowner must:

  • Have all required annual preventive maintenance performed exclusively by Mechanical Enterprise LLC.
  • Complete one (1) maintenance visit every twelve (12) months from the original installation date.
  • Keep all scheduled maintenance accounts current and paid in full.
  • Maintain the equipment in accordance with the manufacturer's operating guidelines.

The first annual maintenance visit is included at no additional charge and must be completed within the first twelve (12) months following system installation.

2 Failure to maintain warranty coverage

Failure to complete any required annual maintenance with Mechanical Enterprise LLC will immediately terminate the extended labor warranty.

If any annual maintenance is missed, canceled, declined, or performed by another contractor, the warranty will automatically revert to the Standard One-Year Labor Warranty, measured from the original installation date. No reinstatement or prorated extension will be available.

3 Standard one-year labor warranty

If the annual maintenance requirements are not met, Mechanical Enterprise LLC warrants its workmanship and labor for one (1) year from the installation date.

The one-year labor warranty covers defects resulting from installation workmanship only and does not extend beyond the initial one-year period.

4 Coverage

The Labor Warranty covers labor required to repair or replace components that have failed under the manufacturer's parts warranty due to normal residential operation. Covered labor includes:

  • Diagnostic labor for covered failures.
  • Removal and replacement of manufacturer-warranted components.
  • Standard labor required to restore system operation.

5 Exclusions

This Labor Warranty does not cover:

  • Manufacturer parts not covered under the manufacturer's warranty.
  • Filters, refrigerant lost due to non-warrantable leaks, batteries, drain cleaning, water treatment, or other routine maintenance items.
  • Cosmetic damage.
  • Damage caused by misuse, abuse, neglect, lack of maintenance, vandalism, flooding, fire, lightning, power surges, freezing, acts of nature, or accidents.
  • Damage resulting from modifications, repairs, or service performed by parties other than Mechanical Enterprise LLC.
  • Electrical service, utility issues, building deficiencies, ductwork not installed by Mechanical Enterprise LLC, plumbing, condensate drainage outside the installed system, or structural conditions.
  • Commercial applications unless specifically stated in writing.

6 Transferability

This warranty is non-transferable unless otherwise approved in writing by Mechanical Enterprise LLC.

7 Manufacturer warranty

This Labor Warranty is separate from and does not modify the manufacturer's equipment warranty. Manufacturer parts coverage is subject to the manufacturer's registration requirements, limitations, and terms.

8 Limitation of liability

Mechanical Enterprise LLC's liability is limited solely to the labor covered under this warranty. Mechanical Enterprise LLC shall not be responsible for incidental, indirect, special, or consequential damages, including property damage, loss of use, utility costs, or loss of comfort.
